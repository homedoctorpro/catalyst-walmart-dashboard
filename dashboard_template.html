Traited = stores on the SKU's official Walmart assortment/planogram (authorized to carry it) — the true "should be in" base. In feed = every store the weekly report lists for the SKU, which also includes non-traited stores that dropped the listing but still show up (usually at zero inventory). Holding inventory = the subset physically stocking it now (on-hand > 0). A zero-on-hand store that is traited is a real out-of-stock; a zero-on-hand store that is non-traited is a lost listing, not an OOS — the Authorization panel below splits the two. Each weekly report carries a rolling ~25-week (≈6-month) forward supply plan — scheduled inbound orders (Order Each Qty) by arrival date, per SKU. This tab tracks how that plan changes snapshot to snapshot. The total-book line shifts partly because the window rolls forward one week each report (it drops the nearest arrival week and adds one ~6 months out).

ⓘ Snapshots jump from Week 2 → Week 12: Walmart only attached the detailed by-SKU Supply Plan sheet at Week 2 (the launch buy) and again from Week 12 onward. Weeks 3–11 carried only an aggregate Forecast & Supply summary, whose weekly figure is lead-time-distorted and not a clean weekly rate, so it's intentionally not plotted here. Sources & Methodology

Named-retailer store counts
Company 10-K filings, investor presentations, and corporate fact sheets where available (Walmart, Target, Costco, Sam's Club, Tractor Supply, Kroger, Albertsons, Publix, BJ's, PetSmart, Petco, Pet Supplies Plus, Dollar General, Dollar Tree, Five Below, Home Depot, Lowe's, etc., FY2025 filings). Private chains and co-ops use the most recent published number from company websites or trade press (Wegmans, H-E-B, Hy-Vee, Hollywood Feed, Pet Supermarket, Earthwise Pet, Ace Hardware, Do it Best, True Value, Menards, Meijer, Rural King, Bomgaars, Atwoods, Fleet Farm, Murdoch's, etc.).
"Remaining Pet Specialty" (~7,000 doors)
Long-tail estimate of regional pet specialty chains (Pet Food Express, Mud Bay, Pet Club, Bentley's Pet Stuff, Centinela Feed, Healthy Spot, Pet Pros, Bark Avenue, etc.) plus independent pet stores. Reconciled against APPA industry overviews, Pet Industry Distributors Association tallies, and independent-pet-retailer trade groups (~9,000–12,000 total US pet specialty doors).
"Remaining Grocery" (~10,000 doors)
Regional supermarket chains (SpartanNash banners, Save Mart, Schnucks, Big Y, Stater Bros, Brookshire's, Ingles, Tops, Weis, Bashas', Smart & Final, Lunds & Byerlys, etc.) plus independent supermarkets. Cross-checked against FMI's Food Industry Almanac and Progressive Grocer's annual chain rankings.
Channel default U/S/W
Starting estimates indexed off Walmart's actual Catalyst U/S/W (this dashboard's Metrics tab) and channel-level pet-litter velocity benchmarks. Mass 1.0 · Pet Specialty 2.0 · Club 3.0 · Farm & Rural 1.5 · Grocery 0.5 · Hardware/Home 0.35 · Drug 0.2 · Dollar/Value 0.3. All editable; override per retailer in the U/S/W column.
Unit economics
$10 wholesale and $20 retail per unit — blended assumption across 15 lb and 34 lb SKUs for opportunity sizing. Actual Walmart wholesale is $9.18 (15 lb) and $12.98 (34 lb) per the Forecast tab's assumptions panel.
Walmart sales data
All other tabs (Metrics, Monthly, Sales Map, OOS, Store Data, Inventory, Endcap, Trial & Repeat, Forecast) are sourced from Walmart's weekly Luminate / Retail Link reports — the 14 Excel files in this repo (202601–202614).
Store counts and estimates current as of 2026-05. The Retailers tab is for opportunity sizing and account planning — not a guarantee of distribution. Adjust U/S/W per retailer in the table to model your own assumptions; bumping a row to "Currently In" turns it green.
